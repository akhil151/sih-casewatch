# PROJECT: NEXUS — AICTE Institution Complaint Intelligence & Coordination Platform

Act as a senior full-stack engineer, product designer, database architect and QA engineer. Build a functional, polished prototype for the following national-level hackathon problem statement.

## 1. Problem statement

**ID:** 26264  
**Title:** Integrated Dashboard for Monitoring and Tracking Institution-Related Complaint Cases Across AICTE Bureaus/Cells.

AICTE receives complaints concerning technical education institutions through different bureaus, including the Regulation Bureau (RB) and Public Grievance Redressal Cell (PGRC). The lack of a consolidated view makes it difficult to identify related cases, understand procedural progression, prevent unnecessary duplicate actions, track pending complaints and coordinate across bureaus.

NEXUS must provide an evidence-first, institution-centric complaint intelligence and coordination dashboard.

## 2. Core architectural principles

Follow these principles throughout implementation:

1. Original bureau records remain authoritative. NEXUS stores source-attributed observations and consolidated read models.
2. A shared institution does not automatically imply a duplicate complaint.
3. A shared matter does not automatically imply an unnecessary duplicate action.
4. Legitimate regulatory referrals, committee stages, proceedings, inspections and appeals must be distinguishable from potential parallel actions.
5. AI-generated conclusions must never silently become official facts.
6. Every simulated record, source and workflow must be clearly identified as simulated.
7. No fake AICTE API, live data feed or official integration may be claimed as operational.
8. Prefer a small, reliable, testable implementation over unnecessary microservices or infrastructure.

### D. Coordination Workbench

This is the core differentiating feature.

Create an evidence-first workflow for investigating potential cross-bureau overlap.

A coordination item should include:

- Institution.
- Involved source cases.
- Involved bureaus.
- Potential relationship type.
- Matching evidence.
- Conflicting evidence.
- Missing information.
- Data freshness.
- Rule/model version, where applicable.
- Review status.
- Assigned reviewer.
- Review rationale.
- Creation and update timestamps.

Support these review decisions:

- Confirmed same matter.
- Related but distinct matters.
- Legitimate procedural progression.
- Potential unnecessary parallel action.
- Insufficient evidence.
- Dismissed suggestion.

Require a rationale for consequential review decisions.

A confirmed same-matter relationship must not automatically mark official actions as duplicates.

For the initial version, use deterministic rules and explicit evidence. Examples:

- Same verified institution and a shared formal referral reference.
- Same institution, category and overlapping dates without an established procedural relationship.
- Different institutions with similar names, which should be flagged for identity verification rather than merged.

Treat these as candidate-generation rules, not definitive conclusions. Make their limitations visible.

Do not claim an AI model has been trained or evaluated if it has not.

### E. Regulatory Timeline

Support relationship types such as:

- Formal referral.
- Proceeding stage of.
- Action derived from.
- Appeal of.
- Potential same matter.
- Confirmed same matter.
- Related but distinct.

Display a chronological timeline with source references and timestamps.

Include a simulated example in which PGRC receives a complaint and a subsequent RB action follows a documented referral. NEXUS must recognize the relationship as possible legitimate regulatory progression rather than automatically flagging it as duplicate work.

Mark simulated regulatory workflows clearly. Do not represent an illustrative workflow as a verified live AICTE case.

## 7. Database design

Create a normalized PostgreSQL schema with appropriate foreign keys, unique constraints, indexes and timestamps.

Include tables equivalent to:

- profiles
- institutions
- institution_aliases
- data_sources
- source_cases
- source_observations
- complaint_categories
- case_assignments
- case_actions
- proceedings
- case_relationships
- coordination_episodes
- coordination_items
- coordination_reviews
- audit_events
- ingestion_runs
- reconciliation_issues
- rule_versions

Adjust the schema if necessary, but preserve the domain distinctions.

Important rules:

- Use UUIDs for internal primary keys.
- Enforce uniqueness on the source identifier within the relevant source system.
- Preserve source facts and their provenance.
- Do not use AI-generated relationships as authoritative source relationships.
- Record review decisions and their rationale.
- Prevent unauthorized users from reading or modifying restricted records.
- Make audit events append-only for normal application roles.
- Add indexes for case ID, institution ID, status, bureau, received date and relationship lookup.
- Use transactions for multi-step review decisions and other consistency-sensitive operations.

Create version-controlled SQL migrations and a documented seed script.
